Exercise 6.

The deltoid curve is formed by a point on a circle of radius \(a\) as it rolls around the inside of a circle of radius \(3a\text{.}\) It has parametric equations:
\begin{align*} x &= 2a \cos t + a \cos 2t\\ y &= 2a \sin t - a \sin 2t \end{align*}
over the interval \(0\leq t \leq 2\pi\text{.}\) See Figure 6.

(a)

Show that the area enclosed by the deltoid curve is equal to
\begin{equation*} A = -2 \int_0^\pi y(t) x'(t) \,dt\text{.} \end{equation*}
