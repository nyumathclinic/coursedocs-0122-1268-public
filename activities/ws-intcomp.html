    Worksheet Worksheet for Section 8.3: The Integral and Comparison Tests
    

    Objectives

    • Use the integral test to decide if a series converges.
      🔗
      🔗
    • Use the comparison test to decide if a series converges.
      🔗
      🔗
    • Use the limit comparison test to decide if a series converges.
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗

    1.

    Use the integral test to determine the convergence or divergence of \(\displaystyle \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{\sqrt[4]{n}}.\)
    🔗
    🔗

    2.

    Use the integral test to determine the convergence or divergence of \(\displaystyle \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n^4}.\)
    🔗
    🔗

    3.

    For what values of \(p\) is the series \(\displaystyle \sum_{n=2}^\infty \frac{1}{n (\ln n)^p}\) convergent?
    🔗
    🔗

    4.

    Determine the convergence of the series
    \begin{equation*} \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{n^2 - 1}{3n^4 + 1}. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    5.

    Determine the convergence of the series \(\sum_{n=2}^\infty \frac{3}{\sqrt{2n-2}}\) .
    🔗
    🔗

    6.

    Determine the convergence of the series
    \begin{equation*} \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{n^2 + 1}{3n^4 - 1}. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    7.

    Determine the convergence of the series
    \begin{equation*} \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{4 + 3^n}{2^n}. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    8.

    Determine the convergence of the series
    \begin{equation*} \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1 + \sin n}{10^n}. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    9.

    Do problems 4–7 with the Limit Comparison Test.
    🔗
    🔗

    10.

    Suppose \(f(x)\) is a function that is continuous, positive, and decreasing on \([1,\infty)\text{.}\) Let \(f(k) = a_k\) for all \(k \geq 1\text{.}\) Suppose \(n\) is an integer. Rank the values \(\int_1^n f(x) \, dx\text{,}\) \(\sum_{k=1}^{n-1} a_k\text{,}\) and \(\sum_{k=2}^{n}a_k\) in increasing order.
    🔗
    🔗

    11.

    With the same conditions as in problem 10, rank the values \(\int_n^\infty f(x) \, dx\text{,}\) \(\sum_{k=n+1}^\infty a_k\text{,}\) and \(\int_{n+1}^\infty f(x) \, dx\) in increasing order.
    🔗
